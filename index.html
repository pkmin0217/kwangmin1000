The Science Behind the Plan

Our recommendations are powered by the Mifflin-St Jeor Equation, widely considered one of the most accurate methods for estimating Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) in clinical settings.

  • BMR: The energy expended while at rest in a neutrally temperate environment.
  • TDEE: Total Daily Energy Expenditure, which accounts for your activity level.
  • Caloric Deficit/Surplus: Carefully adjusted based on your goal to ensure sustainable results.

Expert Health Guides

Essential Protein Intake

Protein is the building block of muscle. For fat loss, aim for 1.6g to 2.2g of protein per kg of body weight to preserve lean mass.

The Power of Hydration

Drinking water can boost metabolism by 24–30% over 1–1.5 hours, helping you burn more calories.

Sustainable Eating Tips

Focus on whole foods. High-volume, low-calorie vegetables help you feel full while staying within your calorie targets.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are these calculations?

While the Mifflin-St Jeor formula is highly reliable, individual metabolism can vary by 10-15%. Use this as a starting point and adjust based on your progress.

Can I change my preferences later?

Absolutely! You can re-run the calculator as your weight or fitness goals evolve.
