
# Blueprint: Health Diet Recommendation Site

## Overview

This document outlines the design and development plan for a web-based health diet recommendation application. The application will provide users with personalized diet plans based on their health goals and dietary preferences.

## Features

*   **User Input Form:** A form for users to input their health goals (e.g., weight loss, muscle gain), dietary restrictions (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free), and other relevant information.
*   **Diet Recommendation Engine:** A (initially simple) logic to generate a diet plan based on user input.
*   **Display Results:** A clear and easy-to-read display of the recommended diet plan.

## Design and Style

*   **Layout:** A clean and modern single-page application layout.
*   **Color Scheme:** A palette of healthy and energetic colors.
*   **Typography:** Clear and readable fonts.
*   **Interactivity:** Smooth transitions and interactive elements.
